# Plan: v0.2 test experience

Status: **draft, collecting ideas** (not started). Owner: @puttyman.

v0.1.0 works end to end. This round is about how a test *feels*: a clear order, a more
striking hero, user-chosen test length, tests that scale to fast LANs, and a recognisable
icon.

## 1. Clear test order: latency → download → upload → packet loss

**Today.** `standard` follows Cloudflare's interleaved sequence (download 100 kB, upload
100 kB, packet loss, upload 1 MB, download 10 MB, upload 10 MB, ...). The hero flips
between download and upload every few seconds, packet loss runs in the middle, and the
phase timeline fills out of order (Packet loss ticked early, Download half-filled while
upload runs).

**Change.** Reorder every size-based profile into four blocks:

1. **Latency**: idle pings (1 + 20).
2. **Download**: all sizes, smallest to largest, with loaded-latency probes during it.
3. **Upload**: all sizes, smallest to largest, with loaded-latency probes.
4. **Packet loss**: WebRTC, last.

- The phase timeline then fills strictly left to right, which also fixes the earlier
  timeline issue.
- Ramp halting stays per direction: a direction stops growing sizes once a request takes
  longer than the cut-off.
- Results stay comparable with v0.1 runs: same sizes and counts, only the order changes.
  The run records the profile version so history can tell them apart.

**Effort:** small (`model::profile`, tests). **Risk:** low.

## 2. Futuristic hero animation with physics

**Today.** Thin streaks drift down (download) or up (upload), with density following speed.

**Change.** A particle system with simple physics on the existing canvas:

- **Download**: particles spawn above the hero, fall under gravity with slight drag, and
  speed up as throughput rises. They glow briefly and bounce softly off the hero number
  before fading.
- **Upload**: particles rise like sparks (buoyancy instead of gravity) from the bottom
  edge and trail off upward.
- **Speed → energy**: throughput on a log scale drives spawn rate, initial velocity,
  trail length and brightness. A fast link looks like a storm, a slow one like a drizzle.
- **Latency pings**: a ring pulse travels out from the number and back, one per probe.
- **Packet loss**: a short burst of dots; lost packets flash and drop out.
- **Finish**: particles settle into a calm ambient drift.
- **Look**: additive blending for a soft glow, series colours (blue download, pink upload),
  and a subtle grid/scanline backdrop in dark mode for the "futuristic" feel.
- **Unchanged guarantees**: it never slows the measurement (it reads a signal, capped
  particle count, lightweight mode if frames drop, pauses in hidden tabs), and reduced
  motion still shows a static gradient.

**Effort:** medium (`components/motion/packet_flow.rs`). **Risk:** low; visual only.

## 3. Duration-based tests (setting) — done

A **Test length** setting next to Profile: **Automatic** (default, the size-based profiles)
or **Timed**, which replaces the profile choice with a **Duration** slider from 10 s to
5 min (stops at 10, 15, 20, 30, 45 s, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5 min; "Download 30 s, then upload 30 s").
Remembered per device, like the profile.

- **Profile** `timed` (`Profile::timed(direction_ms)`, SPEC §5.3): idle latency (1 + 20
  probes), continuous download, continuous upload, packet loss. It reuses the group test's
  timed steps (back-to-back requests sized to finish on time, loaded latency throughout).
  Timed steps are exempt from the 60 s time cap, so a 5 min test runs in full.
- **Results**: `download_bps` / `upload_bps` stay the p90; timed runs add
  `download_peak_bps`, `download_avg_bps`, `upload_peak_bps`, `upload_avg_bps`. Average =
  bytes over the window after its first second; peak = the best 1 s from the live byte
  counter (read every 100 ms). Every run now stores a throughput-over-time series (at most
  600 points per direction), shown as a chart on the run page.
- **UI**: the hero counts down ("Download · 23 s left") with a small countdown ring; the
  timeline fills with time; tiles read "Peak 941 · Avg 902 Mbps"; the live chart thins long
  runs.
- **Test together**: the organizer picks the length with the same slider before Start
  (default: their Timed length, or the server default with Automatic). `GroupCreate` /
  `GroupStart` carry an optional `direction_ms`; `GroupInvite` and `GroupUpdate` carry the
  length, so invitees see "Test together · 1 min each way". The group result shows each
  device's average and peak.
- **Cap**: instead of a new `--max-test-seconds`, the server clamps group lengths to
  2 s – 5 min (the same range as `--group-test-seconds`). Solo timed tests are client-driven
  and bounded by the profile's own clamp.
- **Tests**: `?timed_ms=N` (test-only) shortens a timed run below the slider's minimum so
  the Playwright runs stay under 10 s.

## 4. More icons

Use one consistent open-licensed set (proposal: **Lucide**, ISC licence, inlined as SVG so
nothing loads from a CDN) across:

- the metric tiles (download ↓, upload ↑, latency, jitter, packet loss)
- the phase timeline steps and the quality-score rows (streaming, gaming, video call)
- the settings drawer rows and the history row actions
- device kinds in the Devices panel (phone, tablet, laptop, desktop, TV)

**Effort:** small to medium. **Risk:** low.

## 5. Tests that scale to fast networks (auto 1 GB and 2 GB) — done

Upload gained 1 GB and 2 GB steps, download a 2 GB step (both directions now end 250 MB →
1 GB → 2 GB). Ramp halting gates them: a size runs only if the previous one finished in
under 1 s, so 1 GB runs above about 2 Gbps and 2 GB above about 8 Gbps; slower links are
unchanged. The phase label says "Fast link · Download 1 GB × 2". The direction budget (25 s)
and the server's 2 GB per-request cap still apply.

## 6. A favicon that represents the app

**Today.** A plain gradient circle.

**Change.** A **speed gauge** mark (an arc with a needle) in the brand gradient, clear at
16 px and on light or dark tabs.

- A full set: `favicon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, 32/180/192/512 px PNGs, maskable PWA icon.
- The same mark in the header logo and the README.

**Effort:** small. **Risk:** none.

## 7. Make Test together the showcase feature

Testing many devices at once is rare among speed tests, so it should stand out in the app
and the docs.

- **README and Docker Hub**: a dedicated "Test together" section near the top (done in
  v0.1, with the invite and result screenshots).
- **Polish found in the current screenshots**:
  - The 3-2-1 countdown overlaps the faded hero text; give it a clean full-hero overlay.
  - The invite toast is low-contrast grey; make it a clear card with the organizer's name,
    the test length and a prominent **Join** button.
  - Live lanes show "0.00 Mbps" for a member that hasn't ramped up yet; show "Starting…"
    until its first sample.
- **In the app**: a short empty-state hint in the Devices panel ("Open this page on
  another device to test together") and a "How it works" link.
- **New screenshots and a short animated GIF** of the whole flow (devices appear → invite →
  countdown → live lanes → result), retaken with the v0.2 visuals, in light and dark.

**Effort:** small to medium. **Risk:** low.

## Order of work

1. Clear test order (1, released in 0.1.1) and auto 1 GB / 2 GB (5): done.
2. Duration-based tests (3): done.
3. Hero physics (2), icons (4), favicon (6) and Test together polish (7): UI-only, can run
   in parallel with 3. Retake all screenshots last.

Each step ships with unit tests, the Playwright suite in three browsers, and screenshots;
v0.2.0 is released through the same workflow.

## Open questions

None at the moment.

## Decided

- Test length: Automatic, or a timed slider from 10 s up to 5 min (§3).
- Test together: the organizer chooses the length for everyone (§3).
- Fast links: escalate to 1 GB, and to 2 GB on very fast links (§5).
- Favicon: a speed gauge (§6).
- Test together is the showcase feature (§7).
